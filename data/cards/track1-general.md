# Track 1: General Capability

Logical datasets: MMLU-Pro, HellaSwag, GSM8K, HumanEval, and IFEval.

The normalized layer stores either ordered multiple-choice options with a gold
index or a generation instruction with references and optional unit tests.
Prompt formatting and answer parsing are intentionally deferred to the Task
layer. Full data remains subject to each upstream license and dataset card.
