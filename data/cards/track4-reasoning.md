# Track 4: Reasoning Regimes

Logical datasets: GSM8K and MATH-500 for chain-style reasoning; WinoGrande and
Path-Star for planning-style reasoning.

Normalized records explicitly label the reasoning regime and store a direct
answer. Multiple-choice planning samples also preserve ordered choices and the
gold index. Few-shot or chain-of-thought variants are separate Task-layer
experiments and do not alter these records.
